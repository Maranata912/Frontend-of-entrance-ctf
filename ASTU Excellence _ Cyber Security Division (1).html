Rules

Every member agrees to these rules. Breaking them ends your membership.

  1. Ethical use only. Practise only in the club labs or on systems you own or have written permission to test.
  2. No attacks on ASTU or any real system. Never target the university network, accounts or other students.
  3. Respect privacy. Do not access, copy or share anyone's personal data.
  4. Report, do not exploit. If you find a real vulnerability, tell the cyber head first.
  5. Share knowledge. Help teammates learn and give credit for other people's work.
  6. Follow the law. Ethiopian and international cyber laws apply to everything we do.

FAQ

Do I need experience to join?

No. Start with the Cyber Starter path. We teach from the basics.

Is it free?

Membership is open to ASTU students. Contact us for details about fees, if any.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop and curiosity. Lab machines and practice targets are provided.

Will you teach me to hack real systems?

No. We teach ethical hacking on authorised targets only. See the Rules.
